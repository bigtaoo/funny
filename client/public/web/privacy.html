3. Payment Processing

Who processes your payment depends on where you play. In both cases we never see or store your card number or billing address — the processor holds those directly, and we receive only the amount, the product, and a transaction identifier.

  • On the web: payments are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited, our Merchant of Record. Paddle's privacy policy applies to payment data: paddle.com/legal/privacy.
  • In the iOS app: all purchases go through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple is the seller of record, handles the payment and your billing relationship, and its privacy policy applies to that data: apple.com/legal/privacy. We receive Apple's receipt in order to deliver what you bought; billing questions and refunds for iOS purchases are handled by Apple, not by us.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Paddle (web) / Apple (iOS app) — payment processing (see §3)
  • Google AdMob — rewarded video ads in the mobile app, requested as non-personalised ads only. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites: how Google uses data from partner sites
  • Infrastructure providers — cloud hosting (servers located in the EU/EEA where possible)
  • Legal authorities — if required by law

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to save your session token, game settings and a pseudonymous device identifier. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

5a. Analytics and Your Choice

  • EU / EEA: analytics are off until you agree — in a first-launch dialog, or a non-blocking prompt on portals such as CrazyGames. Saying no plays exactly the same.
  • Elsewhere, including the UK and the US: analytics are on by default to improve the game. We tell you so when you start playing; on portals, no analytics data leaves your device before that notice has been shown. In the UK this relies on the PECR exemption for statistical purposes.
  • Everywhere: you can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Analytics. We then stop collecting analytics events, stop recording you in the service statistics, and erase the record of which days you played.

Anonymous step counts carry no identifier and are not personal data; they are counted for every player.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in-game (Settings → Delete Account), or ask us to by email (see §7).

After you delete your account, a 7-day grace period applies: logging back in during that time restores it. Once it ends, an automated job erases your data across all our services, normally within a few hours — login credentials, profile, saves, inventory, coin balance, friends, private messages, mail, and your presence in the game world. Your account record is reduced to an internal identifier with no personal data. If an auction trade of yours is still in progress (someone has bid on your listing, or you are the highest bidder), erasure waits until it settles so the other player is not left out of pocket.

Messages you posted in family/sect/world channels are deleted; family or sect leadership passes to another member (or the group is disbanded if no one is left). Other players' match history and battle reports are kept with your name removed. Reports you filed about other players are kept without your identity so they can still be reviewed.

Transaction records are kept for 10 full calendar years (counted from the end of the year of the transaction) to comply with accounting and tax law, and are deleted after that; after account deletion they are no longer linked to your identity and raw receipts are removed. Analytics events are deleted together with the account. Erased data disappears from our rolling database backups as they expire (7 days by default).

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR you have the right to: access your data, rectify inaccurate data, erase your data ("right to be forgotten"), restrict or object to processing, and data portability. To object to analytics and service statistics, simply turn analytics off in Settings. To exercise any right, email support@gamestao.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures including HTTPS, hashed passwords, and access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated via in-game notice or email.
