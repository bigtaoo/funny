Frequently asked

My progress is gone / I'm on a new device

Progress belongs to your account, not to the device. Log in with the same email or username and password you signed up with, and everything follows you across devices and platforms. If you played without ever creating an account, that save is local to that install and there is no way for us to recover or transfer it.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the game: tap your name at the top of the lobby to open Profile, then Account → Delete Account, and confirm. For 7 days after that, simply logging in again restores the account; once those 7 days pass, deletion is permanent and your progress and purchases cannot be brought back. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

How do I get a refund?

Bought inside the iOS app: refunds are handled by Apple — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Bought on the website: see our Refund Policy. We cannot refund an App Store purchase ourselves, and Apple cannot refund a website purchase.

What are the odds on the random item packs?

Every draw rate, and every pity guarantee, is published inside the game: Store tab → Gacha → the i button at the top right of the banner. Nothing is hidden and nothing changes silently — rolls are resolved on our servers, not on your device.

Someone is harassing me in chat

Tap the player's name to open their profile, then Report or Block. Blocking takes effect immediately for you; reports go into a review queue and can lead to a warning, a chat mute, or a ban. If it is urgent, email us with the player's name and roughly when it happened.

I found a bug

Use Feedback on the lobby screen — it sends straight to us and already carries your account, so we can look at the right save. For anything that needs a screenshot, email works better.

How old do I have to be?

13, and under 18 you need a parent or guardian's consent. The game asks you to declare your age the first time you open it. See section 2 of the Terms of Service.

Privacy, data access, and GDPR requests

What we collect and why is in the Privacy Policy. To access, correct, export, or erase your data, email us from the address on the account — we will not act on a request we cannot tie to an account.

Can I play in my own language?

The game is fully translated into English, Deutsch, and 中文 — every screen, not just the menus. It follows your device language on first launch, and you can switch it any time in Profile.
